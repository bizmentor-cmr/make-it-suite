# Higgsfield Reviews & Product Details

Visit Website

Higgsfield is an AI-native creative suite. It combines proprietary tools for video, character consistency, and ad creation with access to a growing library of AI models, under one subscription.

Each tool handles a different stage of production. Cinema Studio is for cinematic video: camera and lighting control, multi-shot scenes, and characters and locations you can reuse across shots. Marketing Studio turns a product URL or image into a finished ad, in UGC, unboxing, tutorial, or TV spot format, with lip sync. Canvas is a node board where you chain models together, image to video to edit, and save the workflow as a template. AI Influencer builds a consistent virtual character through a visual builder instead of prompts. Supercomputer takes a plain-language brief, breaks it into steps, picks a model for each one, and returns finished assets in the same chat. It also connects to Slack, Google Drive, Notion, Gmail, and TikTok, so a task can pull in references and publish the result.

Soul is the in-house image model family, built for cinematic, fashion, and editorial work. Soul ID sits on top of it: train a character once from photos, and the same face holds across every image and video. Teams also get access to models including Sora, Google Veo, Kling, Seedance, Nano Banana, and GPT Image, so they can compare outputs and switch models without leaving the platform or paying for separate subscriptions.

Generation runs outside the platform too. MCP connects to Claude and other AI agents, CLI and Skills work inside coding agents such as Claude Code and Cursor, and plugins run in Figma, Photoshop, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve. No API key required. The Academy covers the tools in structured courses.

Everything runs in the browser, with credit-based and unlimited generation options across Individual and Business plans. More than 3.6 million people have created over 200 million videos on the platform.
