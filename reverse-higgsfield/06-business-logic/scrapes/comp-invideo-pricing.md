### What is a credit, and how is it charged?

Credits work like currency on our platform and are used for video creation, generative models and across other features, not for downloading content. Generative models are priced differently. All models are available at their original API pricing. You can use your credits however you like across the platform. We reserve the right to update credit costs at any time, without prior notice.

Heads up: Unused credits don't roll over to the next month.
