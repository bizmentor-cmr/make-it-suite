We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions
What makes this platform different from other AI video tools?Our platform unites all leading generative models in one secure space built for enterprise use. Teams can manage content, collaborate, and scale production safely with full control over data and permissions.
Do I need technical expertise to use the platform?Not at all. The platform is designed for creators, marketers, and enterprises alike. Intuitive workflows and guided onboarding make it easy to start producing videos right away.
Can multiple teams collaborate in real time?Yes. You can assign roles, leave comments, track approvals, and manage multiple projects simultaneously, all within shared folders and structured teams.
How secure is the platform?Security is enterprise-grade. We're SOC 2 - aligned, ensuring your data, assets, and AI workflows are protected under the highest standards.
What happens after I request a demo?Once you request a demo, our team will reach out to schedule a personalized session. We'll walk you through key features, use cases, and help tailor the setup to your organization's needs.
Can we influence product updates or request new features?Yes. As part of our continuous partnership model, enterprise clients can provide feedback, join roadmap discussions, and get early access to upcoming capabilities.
