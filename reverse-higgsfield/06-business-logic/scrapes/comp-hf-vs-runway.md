## What Is the Difference?

Higgsfield is an AI-native creative suite with 35+ models and tools. On top of its video models, it adds Soul ID for a reusable character identity, Marketing Studio for URL-to-ad production, Cinema Studio 4.0 for camera and lens settings, Lipsync Studio for spoken video, Ads Studio for image ads, and Supercomputer for production from a single chat.

Runway is a generation platform with an editing layer. Aleph 2.0 edits existing video from a text prompt, Act-Two transfers a performance, and the platform generates music, voice, and sound effects natively. Runway also builds its own model, Gen-4.5.

The two platforms differ in what they build around the models. Higgsfield adds more models and production tools and a reusable character identity. Runway adds video-to-video editing and its own Gen-4.5 model. If your workflow ends at the generated clip and editing happens elsewhere, Runway's post-production layer may not justify the credit math. If your workflow starts at character consistency and ends at a delivered campaign asset, Higgsfield covers more of that pipeline natively.

## What Only Higgsfield Has

[Soul ID](https://higgsfield.ai/character/upload) trains a persistent character identity from reference photos. Upload 20+ images of a real person, train the identity in a few minutes. From that point you can reuse the trained identity to generate videos and new images of that character, which helps keep the same face across later generations without re-uploading references each time.

[Marketing Studio](https://higgsfield.ai/marketing-studio/product)  takes a product URL or photos and builds campaign-ready video and image assets from 1,500+ templates across six categories: Product Shots, Ads, Marketplace, Posters, UGC Videos, and Motion. For anything a template doesn't cover, you can describe the ad in a prompt. Brief writing, asset generation, and formatting for each platform happen inside one tool.

Cinema Studio 4.0 sets genre, camera, lens, tempo, emotion, colour palette, era, lighting, and aperture as controls, with 30+ camera movement presets, clips up to 30 seconds, and up to 50 references.

[LipSync Studio](https://higgsfield.ai/lipsync-studio) turns a character image into a talking video or adds lip sync to existing footage, with eight models to choose from and the same credit balance.

[Supercomputer](https://higgsfield.ai/supercomputer) is Higgsfield's AI agent for end-to-end production from a single chat. Describe the deliverable, the agent plans the steps, picks the right models, shows the credit cost before running, and executes on approval.

## What Only Runway Has

**Gen-4.5** is Runway's own video model. At 12 credits per second (60 credits for 5 seconds), a 10-second clip costs 120 credits. At Standard ($15/month, 625 credits), that is about 5 clips per month. At Pro ($35/month, 2,250 credits), it is about 18 clips.

**Act-Two** transfers a performance onto a character.

**Ruby** converts SDR video into HDR, expanding its brightness and color range. It supports professional formats such as ProRes and EXR and costs 20 credits per second.

**Characters** creates talking-character videos from an image and a text script or audio recording, with synchronized speech and animation.

**Enhance Frame Rate** generates intermediate frames to convert video to a different frame rate, up to 120 fps. It preserves duration and resolution and costs 1 credit per 2 seconds.

**Aleph 2.0** edits an existing clip from a text prompt and uses credits, at 140 credits per 5 seconds.

## The Value Question for High-Volume Generators

Credits aren’t equivalent across the two platforms, so compare the cost of the same model, duration, and resolution on each platform’s current rate card. As two anchors, a 10-second Gen-4.5 clip on Runway costs 120 credits, about $2.88 on Standard ($15 for 625 credits) and about $1.20 on Max ($95 for 9,500). On Higgsfield, a 10-second Cinema Studio 4.0 clip at 720p with native audio costs 70 credits as of October 2026, about $3.43 at the Plus rate ($49 for 1,000 credits). These are different models, so they illustrate how each platform prices credits and don’t show which one is cheaper for the same shot.

For high-volume work, plan limits matter too. Runway Standard, Pro, and Max allow 5, 15, and 20 parallel generations, and Max rolls unused credits over for one month. Higgsfield Plus allows up to 6 video and 8 image generations at once, Ultra lists unlimited paid parallel generations, and individual-plan credits reset each month. Additional generations or alternatives use more credits on both platforms.

Higgsfield suits creators and teams who need a reusable character identity, ad tools, and a wider production pipeline. Runway suits productions that prioritize Gen-4.5 and video-to-video editing. Choose based on what the production requires.

## Who Actually Benefits From Each Platform?

**Choose Higgsfield if:**

- The same character needs to appear consistently across multiple clips, models, or sessions
- Your production includes native audio, spoken video, or multilingual content
- You want ad production tools, camera control, and character consistency inside the same subscription
- You are building a full creative pipeline from character to campaign asset

**Choose Runway if:**

- Gen-4.5 is your primary model and cinematic quality on proprietary models is the priority
- Post-production editing, compositing, and Motion Brush-level frame control matter as much as generation
- Your workflow does not require persistent character consistency across sessions
- You are comfortable handling audio in a separate step after generation

## Higgsfield or Runway: Which One Is Right for Your Workflow?

Choosing between Higgsfield and Runway comes down to what you're building around the model, not the model itself. Both platforms carry the same shared generation stack. Higgsfield wraps it in a full creative suite: [Soul ID](https://higgsfield.ai/character/upload), [Marketing Studio](https://higgsfield.ai/marketing-studio/product), [Cinema Studio](https://higgsfield.ai/generate), and [Supercomputer](https://higgsfield.ai/supercomputer) for teams that need character consistency and production tooling baked in. Runway wraps it in a professional editing environment with Gen-4.5 and Gen-4 Turbo for productions where cinematic quality and frame-level control are the priority. Neither is the wrong choice. The right one depends on where your workflow actually begins and ends.

## Got any questions left?

Do both platforms include Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, WAN 3.0, Veo 3.1, and Gemini Omni Flash 1.1?

Yes. All five are available on both platforms as licensed integrations. Model updates on third-party integrations may arrive later than on native platforms.

Does Runway have a character consistency system like Soul ID?

Runway doesn't document a trained character identity like Soul ID. You add a reference image to each generation, and Runway offers custom voice clones (1 on Pro, 3 on Max)

Which platform is cheaper at entry level?

Both start at $15 a month, Higgsfield Starter (200 credits) and Runway Standard (625 credits). Runway Standard is $12 a month, and credits aren't equivalent across the two platforms.

Does Runway have native audio generation?

Yes. Runway generates music, voice, and sound effects natively, starting with the Standard plan. On Higgsfield, some models such as Veo 3.1 generate audio with the video, and Seed Audio 1.0 and Lipsync Studio cover speech and lip sync.

Is Gen-4.5 available on Higgsfield?

No, Gen-4.5 is Runway's own model and isn't listed on Higgsfield.
