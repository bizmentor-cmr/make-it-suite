# Synthesia Reviews & Product Details

Visit Website

Synthesia is the world's leading AI video platform, trusted by 90% of the Fortune 100. In one platform, teams create with Synthesia Studio and run two-way conversations with AI Avatars through Sessions. Together they scale training, practice, insight, and guidance across the organization.

Create video from any input: Type or paste a script, generate one from a prompt, or turn a PowerPoint, PDF, Word doc, link, or template into a polished, publish-ready video in minutes.

Present with AI Avatars: Choose from a realistic stock avatar library, prompt fully customizable avatars into any outfit, setting, and action, or create a personal avatar from a single photo that looks and sounds like you.

Communicate globally: Localize into 160+ languages with built-in AI translation and dubbing, plus a Translation Glossary that locks key terminology so your message stays accurate everywhere.

Practice with Roleplay Sessions: People step into a real conversation with an Interactive Avatar that talks, asks questions, and pushes back — then get coached and scored in real time, with their skill uplift measured over time, all in one platform.

Gather insight with Survey Sessions: The depth of an interview, the reach of a form. An Avatar asks your questions, probes where it matters, and hands back structured insight — for employee feedback, customer insight, or lead qualification.

Build or embed Interactive Avatars: Use the API to add real-time Interactive Avatars to your own apps and products, or use the fully managed Widget to create a knowledge-grounded agent in Synthesia and embed it on a website.

Engage and measure: Add quizzes, clickable moments, and branching paths to video, and use built-in analytics to see who's watching, where they drop off, and what's driving results.

Enterprise-ready: Synthesia is trusted by the world’s leading organizations for its enterprise-grade security and compliance standards, including SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, ISO 42001, and ISO 27001.

From HR and L&D to Marketing and Sales, Synthesia scales across global teams with managed services, team-collaboration, and the AI governance needed to stay in control.

"Synthesia Makes Video Creation Fast, Intuitive, and High-ROI"

5/5

What do you like best about Synthesia?

I like the simplicity in getting started with Synthesia. It was possible to simply jump right in and begin creating my first video, without receiving any type of training. Creating scenes (whether new or based on a template), editing them, adding a script, and previewing them (including with narration) is quite intuitive. It's actually simpler than creating a PowerPoint slide, with a more interactive and engaging deliverable.

Their AI avatar features are also quite impressive, especially when it comes to selecting and editing an avatar to match the tone, guidelines, and needs of a client brand.

Additionally, customer support is also top notch. They're very kind, patient, and do not stop until your challenge is resolved.

Finally, the ROI in terms of hours saved is unparalleled. As a designer for over 15 years (including video design), the ease of use and quality of work that can be generated in a short period of time allow me to focus more on video strategy and objectives, rather than on the mechanics of designing videos. Review collected by and hosted on G2.com.

What do you dislike about Synthesia?
