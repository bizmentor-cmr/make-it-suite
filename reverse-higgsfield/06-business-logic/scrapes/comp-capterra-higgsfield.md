# Higgsfield

## Higgsfield Overview:

### What Is Higgsfield?

Higgsfield is a cloud-based AI creative suite that helps individuals, teams, and businesses produce images, videos, and voice content using a library of 50+ AI models. Users can generate, edit, and manage creative assets from a single platform without needing technical expertise.

Higgsfield supports workflows for social media content, marketing materials, and visual storytelling. Teams can collaborate on projects, organize assets, and publish content directly from the platform.

Core features include text-to-image and text-to-video generation, voice synthesis, style controls, and a shared asset library. The platform suits freelancers, creative agencies, and marketing teams of all sizes.

### Who Uses Higgsfield?

Individual creators, marketers, agencies, and enterprise teams including Fortune 500 companies. Serves e-commerce, fashion, beauty, food and beverage, advertising, and entertainment industries.
