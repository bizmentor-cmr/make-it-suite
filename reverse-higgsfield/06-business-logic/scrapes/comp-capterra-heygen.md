## Accu-Trax Office Overview

Updated October 2026

AI summary

Accu-Trax Office offers small and midsize businesses tools for managing utility billing and waste management processes. It supports billing and invoicing, customer management, and work order management, allowing organizations to handle essential operational tasks in one place. The software is available via cloud, web, Windows, and Android deployments, providing flexibility for diverse work environments.

## FAQs about Accu-Trax Office

Overview

### What company size and specific industries is Accu-Trax Office built for?

Accu-Trax Office is designed for waste and recycling haulers, drop box providers, and landfills. The target company size is not specified, but the product is aimed at businesses operating in these waste management sectors.

Features and Usability

### What are the key features of Accu-Trax Office?

Accu-Trax Office offers customer management, customer accounts, and customer database tools, plus GPS, location tracking, and vehicle tracking for field operations. It also includes route management, routing, scheduling, dispatch management, work order management, job tracking, inventory management, billing and invoicing, and audit trail capabilities.

Getting Started and Support

### What training and onboarding options does Accu-Trax Office offer?

Accu-Trax Office provides in person training, live online sessions, webinars, documentation, and videos. In person and live online formats support guided instruction, webinars can cover key features or setup steps, documentation offers written reference material, and videos provide visual, self-paced learning for users.

Getting Started and Support

### What customer support options does Accu-Trax Office offer?

Accu-Trax Office provides Email/Help Desk, FAQ/Forum, Knowledge Base, and Chat support. These channels give users multiple ways to find answers or contact support, but no reviewer feedback is available here to describe response times, helpfulness, or common support frustrations.
