Higgsfield and Kling offer credit-based subscriptions for AI video generation, but their pricing structures and production tools differ. Higgsfield brings together 35+ AI models and creative tools, including Kling 3.0, while Kling provides direct access to its own video models, including Kling 4.0. This guide compares plan prices, credits, and the cost of the same clip. All prices are monthly list prices.

## What Is the Difference?

Higgsfield is an AI-native creative suite with 35+ models and tools. Kling 3.0 is currently the Kling model available there, alongside Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1, and others, and Kling 4.0 isn't available on Higgsfield yet. On top of its models, Higgsfield adds Soul ID for a reusable character identity, Marketing Studio for template-based ad production from product images or URLs, Cinema Studio 4.0 for camera and lens settings, Genjutsu for video-to-video edits, 3D Jutsu for editable 3D scenes, and Supercomputer for production from a single chat.

Kling is Kuaishou's AI image and video platform, built around the Kling model family. Kling 4.0 is Kling's latest video model. It generates clips from 3 to 30 seconds at 720p, 1080p, or 4K, supports 10-bit HDR at 1080p and 4K, accepts Omni references and up to 10 keyframes, and generates two-channel stereo audio. Around the models, Kling adds Motion Control, which takes movement from a reference video, multi-shot generation, Canvas, an agent workspace with node editing, and MCP and CLI access.

The main difference lies in the production workflows each platform supports. Higgsfield combines Kling 3.0 with other AI models, character consistency tools, video editing, and ad production under one credit balance. Kling provides direct access to its own model family, including Kling 4.0, and features designed specifically for its video generation ecosystem.

## What Higgsfield Offers Beyond Kling

**Soul ID** trains a persistent character identity from reference photos. Upload 20+ images, train the identity in a few minutes, and reuse it to generate videos and new images of that character, which helps keep the same face across later generations without re-uploading references each time.

**Marketing Studio** takes a product URL or photos and builds campaign-ready video and image assets from 1,500+ templates across six categories: Product Shots, Ads, Marketplace, Posters, UGC Videos, and Motion. For anything a template doesn't cover, you can describe the ad in a prompt.

**Cinema Studio 4.0** provides controls for genre, camera, lens, tempo, emotion, color palette, era, lighting, and aperture. It also offers 30+ camera movement presets, supports clips up to 30 seconds, and accepts up to 50 references.

**Genjutsu** is a video-to-video tool for changing a clip you already have, whether it is real footage or generated video. Motion Transfer takes the motion from a 4 to 30 second reference video and applies it to a new cast, location, or style, and Object Swap replaces selected elements, such as a character, an outfit, or a product, while the rest of the shot stays as filmed. A single generation supports up to 30 reference images.

**3D Jutsu** is a browser workspace where a prompt becomes an editable 3D scene with objects, lighting, cameras, and animation. The scene renders to video of 1 to 60 seconds, or exports as MP4, a static frame, or GLB.

**Supercomputer** is Higgsfield's AI agent for production from a single chat. Describe the deliverable, and the agent plans the steps, picks the models, and shows the credit cost before running.

## What Kling Offers Directly

**Kling 4.0** is Kling’s latest video model. It generates 3 to 30 seconds at 720p, 1080p, and 4K, with 10-bit HDR at 1080p and 4K, and outputs two-channel stereo audio with more accurate lip sync. It accepts up to 10 keyframes and up to 15 combined reference assets, including Omni reference. Kling 4.0 Flash is the faster version, at 3 to 20 seconds and 720p.

The platform also provides access to earlier models, including Kling 3.0 and Kling 3.0 Omni, alongside its latest generation.

**Motion Control** takes movement from a reference video and applies it to a character.

**Element AI Multi-Shot** generates connected shots, with 3 free uses a day.

**Canvas** is a workspace where an agent writes scripts, builds storyboards, and generates images and videos, with node editing.

**MCP and CLI access** lets an agent run Kling for batch generation.

**Free plan:** Kling has a free tier with limited free credits, watermarked output, and no commercial-use rights.

## Who Benefits From Each Platform?

**Choose Higgsfield if:**

- You want 35+ models and tools in one subscription
- You want camera, lens, and lighting controls in Cinema Studio
- The same character needs to appear across multiple clips and models
- You want video-to-video editing, 3D scenes, or ad production inside the same credit balance

**Choose Kling if:**

- Kling models are your main tool and you want direct access to Kling 4.0, including native generation up to 30 seconds.
- You want Motion Control, Element AI Multi-Shot, or Canvas
- You want a free tier to test the platform before you pay.

## Higgsfield vs Kling: Which Platform Should You Choose?

Higgsfield is better suited to creators and teams that need multiple AI models, consistent characters, cinematic controls, and production tools in one workspace. It combines Kling 3.0 with tools such as Soul ID, Marketing Studio, Cinema Studio, Genjutsu, and Supercomputer. Kling is a strong choice for creators who primarily use Kling models and want direct access to Kling 4.0. The better option depends on the cost of your typical generations and the tools your production workflow requires.

## Got any questions left?

Is Higgsfield or Kling cheaper?

Higgsfield Starter costs $15 per month, while Kling Standard starts at $10. However, subscription price alone doesn't determine the cost per video. On the mid-tier plans compared above, the estimated cost of generating the same Kling 3.0 clip is similar on both platforms. The actual cost depends on duration, resolution, and generation settings.

Is Kling 4.0 available on Higgsfield?

Not yet. Higgsfield currently offers Kling 3.0 alongside other models. Kling 4.0 is available on Kling's own platform.

Does Kling have a free plan?

Yes. Kling gives limited credits a day on its free plan, with a watermark and no commercial use.

Do unused credits roll over?

No. Higgsfield's individual subscription credits reset each month, while Kling's monthly subscription credits expire at the end of the billing period.

Which platform has more models?

Higgsfield has 35+ models and tools, including Kling 3.0, while Kling offers its own model family, including Kling 4.0.

Can I use videos from either platform commercially?

Higgsfield permits commercial use of generated outputs under its terms, while Kling offers commercial use on paid plans. Watermark requirements and other restrictions may vary, so review the current terms of each platform before using content in client projects.
