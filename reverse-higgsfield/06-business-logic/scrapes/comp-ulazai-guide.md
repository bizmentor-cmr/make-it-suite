Verify three things on each vendor's current pricing page: whether free credits arrive once or drip in daily, whether free renders carry a watermark, and whether commercial use of a free render is allowed. Those three answers decide if a free tier is usable for ad testing or only for casual clips.

### Which AI model should I use for marketing ads?

Use Veo 3.1 when native generated audio matters, Runway when you need director-style camera control at the provider, and Kling 3.0 for runs up to 15 s at a published per-second rate. Compare two models on the same shot before you commit a budget.
