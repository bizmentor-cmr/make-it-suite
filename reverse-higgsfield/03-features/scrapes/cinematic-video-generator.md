## Got any questions left?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Cinema Studio 4.0?

A full film production inside one agent. You set genre, camera, pacing and cast from one panel — and get a finished, edited scene, not a raw clip.

What's new in 4.0?

Native 4K, generations up to one minute, Montage Pacing, an anti-slop camera pipeline, AI Cast, Cinematic Locations, Soul Cinema Models, multi-model support — and a full team layer: live co-directing, shared elements, Canvas, project briefs and subfolders.

How long and how sharp?

Up to one minute per generation, every frame in native 4K. No upscale tricks — the detail is generated, not stretched.

What is Montage Pacing?

You pick the rhythm — Chaotic, Dynamic, Calm or Single Shot — and the scene arrives already assembled: cuts, rhythm and flow built in. No timeline, no post.

What does "anti-slop" mean?

No plastic skin, no drifting faces, no AI shimmer. The camera pipeline is tuned against everything that makes AI video look AI-generated.

Do characters and locations stay consistent?

Yes. AI Cast actors hold identity, wardrobe and presence across every scene and angle. Locations work the same — come back to the same street, in the same weather, next week.

Can I use models other than Higgsfield's?

Yes. Seedance and other leading video models run natively inside the studio. Pick the engine per shot — your panel, cast and grade stay yours.

How does my team work in one project?

Invite your team and generate together — every shot and every comment lands for everyone in real time. Elements are shared: one person creates a character, location or prop, and the whole crew shoots with it.

Do I need filmmaking experience?

No. If you can describe a scene, you can direct one. Pros get precise control; everyone else gets Mr. Higgs — he'll pick the camera, light the scene or break a script into shots.

How much does it cost?

Cinema Studio 4.0 is available on Higgsfield plans — generations are priced in credits by length, resolution and model. See Pricing for current tiers.
