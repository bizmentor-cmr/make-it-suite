# Onboarding

Copy page

Last updatedOct 6, 2026

Go from an activated workspace to a repeatable production workflow with your team.

Your Customer Success Manager (CSM) helps you set up Enterprise Studio and build a workflow your team can repeat. Reach them in your shared Slack or Microsoft Teams channel or at [enterprise@higgsfield.ai](mailto:enterprise@higgsfield.ai).

## Book your first onboarding call

- After activation your CSM shares a Calendly link in the shared channel for a 30 **-minute call**.
- Invite the workspace Owner, admins or billing contact, and creative leads.

## Prepare for the call

Bring your member list and roles, the groups or sub-workspaces you need, spending limits, your SSO provider, migration needs and your first production goals.

The call walks through **Members**, user groups, **Sub-workspaces**, **Usage** and SSO, and plans creative training.

## Arrange an AI-educator session

1. Tell your CSM the result you need (product visuals, a campaign, UGC, video) and whether you want a demo or a repeatable workflow.
2. Share examples: prompts, references, outputs - including ones that did not work.
3. Name the format and who will attend.
4. Your CSM books the AI Educator; pick a slot from the shared calendar.
5. Follow up with the recording and the shared channel.

## Complete the first week

- Confirm you are in the Enterprise workspace.
- Set group limits, invite the team, assign roles.
- Add sub-workspaces if needed. The Owner sets up SSO with IT.
- Create the first project in the workspace that owns the work.
- Attend the onboarding call and the creative session.

## If something does not look right

**SSO opens a personal workspace** → check the workspace switcher and make sure the same email was invited to the Enterprise workspace.

**An action is unavailable** → check your project role, workspace permissions and group limits.

**Technical issue** → send the page or generation link, the steps, expected vs actual result and a screenshot. See Contact Support.

## Stay in touch

Shared Slack or Microsoft Teams channel, or enterprise@higgsfield.ai. Feature requests and everything go through the same channels.

Next: [create your first project](https://higgsfield.ai/enterprise-help-center/manage-account/projects).
