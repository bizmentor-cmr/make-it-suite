## Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Higgsfield Collab?

Higgsfield Collab is a built-in collaboration space inside Higgsfield where you can create together in shared projects, chat in real time, jump on voice and video calls, share generations, and discover work from the community. Think of it as your creative hub for everything you do with others on Higgsfield.

What is a project in Higgsfield Collab?

A project is a shared space where members can generate together, chat, share assets, and hop on calls. DMs and group conversations are also projects under the hood, so every conversation has the full power of the platform behind it.

Can I make voice or video calls in Higgsfield Collab?

Yes. Any project with two or more members supports audio and video calls. You can join, leave, and mute directly inside the project. A persistent call bar stays visible as you navigate so you never lose your call.

What are Organisations?

Organisations are shared identity groups for teams and communities. A free Org lets members collaborate under a shared name while each person uses their own credits. A paid Org (Team Plan) unlocks shared credits, shared elements, admin controls, and SSO. Every Team Plan workspace is automatically an Org.

Can I share my generations in chat?

Yes. You can send any generation directly into the project chat with one tap using the "Share to Collab" toggle. Your teammates will see the output along with the prompt, model, and progress, so they can react, give feedback, or build on it right away.

What is the community feed?

The community feed shows recommended and trending published work from across the platform. You can discover new creators, explore projects by category, and find inspiration based on your interests and location.

Can people join my projects?

You control who gets in. Projects can be private (only invited members), public (discoverable, view-only for non-members), or open (anyone can join). Public and open projects have a "Request to Join" flow with owner approval, so you always stay in control of your space.
