## Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is AI Inpainting and how does it work?

AI inpainting is a technology that reconstructs missing or damaged parts of an image. Unlike simple cloning tools, our AI image editor analyzes the context of your photo to generate new pixels that blend seamlessly.

How can I remove unwanted objects from my photo?

It's simple. Upload your image, select the "Remove Object" tool, and brush over the item you want to erase (like a tourist, watermark, or power line). Our AI object remover will automatically delete the item and intelligently fill the background so it looks like the object was never there.

Is the AI Photo Editor free to use?

Layer Separation and AI region edits are available on paid plans and use credits. Guided tutorials and promotional offers may include limited free access.

Can I use this tool to add new elements to an image?

Yes. With our Generative Fill feature, you can mask an area and type a text prompt (e.g., "add a vintage car" or "change to sunset"). The Nano Banana Pro engine will generate the new object with correct perspective and lighting to match your original scene.

What file formats do you support?

We support all major image formats, including JPG, PNG, and WebP. You can upload images up to 4K resolution for professional-quality editing and restoration.
