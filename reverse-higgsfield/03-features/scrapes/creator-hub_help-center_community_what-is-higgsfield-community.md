# What is Higgsfield Community?

Copy page

HiggsfieldOct 7, 20263 min

Higgsfield Community is the public space on [higgsfield.ai](http://higgsfield.ai/) where creators publish their works, grow an audience, and earn Community rating. You can share Shots, Open Projects, and Blog posts, follow other creators, like the works you enjoy, and apply for Higgsfield Grants with a published project. Everything you publish appears on your public creator profile at higgsfield.ai/@username.

## What is Higgsfield Community?

Community is how Higgsfield handles sharing and growth: it turns what you create in the studios into a public portfolio. Every creator has a public profile with their published works, follower and like counts, and achievements. As you publish works and take part in contests, you gain Community rating that is displayed on your profile. See [How do rating and achievements work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/community/how-do-achievements-work) for details.

## What can I share in Community?

Community supports three types of content:

- **Open Projects** are projects shared with the result, the project brief, the asset folders, and the models used. Prompts are your choice: at publishing you decide whether they are visible to viewers or stay private.
- **Shots** are single images or videos you publish on their own, outside a project. They appear in the Shots tab on your profile.
- **Blog posts** are write-ups of how you made something: prompts, settings, and pipeline. They appear in the Blog tab on your profile.

Alongside your works, your profile also shows the achievements you've earned; they're covered in [How do rating and achievements work](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/community/how-do-achievements-work) [?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/how-do-rating-and-achievements-work)

To learn how publishing works, see [How do projects work in Higgsfield Community?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/community/how-do-projects-work)

## Where do I find Community?

Select **Community** in the top navigation on [higgsfield.ai](http://higgsfield.ai/), or open the [Community page](https://higgsfield.ai/community) directly. The page is organized into four tabs. Discovery is the front page with selected projects, featured creators, and the Higgsfield Grants banner. Projects and Shots are the full feeds with search and filters, including contest entries, Higgsfield Originals, and your favorites. Creators collects the creators the Higgsfield team highlights. The tabs are covered in [How do projects work in Higgsfield Community?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/community/how-do-projects-work)

## How do likes, follows, and views work?

Select the heart on a Shot to like it; projects are rated with a thumbs up or thumbs down. Follow a creator to keep up with what they publish. Views are counted on published works automatically. Your own totals, followers, following, likes, and views, are displayed on your profile for everyone to see.

## Who can see my published works?

Everything you generate is private by default: a work appears in Community only after you publish it. Once published, anyone can view it, even without a Higgsfield account, while your drafts and unpublished works stay private. Creator profiles themselves are public for everyone. Publishing and visibility are covered in [How do projects work in Higgsfield Community?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/community/how-do-projects-work)

## Got any questions left?

Can other creators see my prompts?

Select Community in the top navigation on higgsfield.ai, or open the [Community page](https://higgsfield.ai/community) directly. It has four tabs: Discovery with selected projects and creators, the full Projects and Shots feeds with search and filters, and Creators with the creators the Higgsfield team highlights. The tabs are covered in [How do projects work in Higgsfield Community?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/community/how-do-projects-work)

What is the rating number on my profile?

That’s your Community rating. You earn points for specific actions like publishing your first Open Project or placing in contests, and the Ranking tab on your profile lists these actions with the credit milestones. See [How do rating and achievements work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/community/how-do-achievements-work)

Do views and likes earn me rating?

Views don’t earn rating, and neither does generating or posting content by itself. Likes don’t add rating directly, but they count toward conditions such as project verification. Rating comes from specific actions described in [How do rating and achievements work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/community/how-do-achievements-work)

What is Higgsfield Grants?

Higgsfield Grants is a program that awards up to 200,000 generation credits to selected published Open Projects. You apply from the [Grants page](https://higgsfield.ai/filmmaker-grant) or from the banner on the Discovery tab of Community.

Do I need a Higgsfield account to browse Community?

No. Published works, creator profiles, rating, and achievements are visible to anyone, even without signing in. You need an account to like works, follow creators, and publish your own.

Is my Assets library public?

No. Assets is your private library, and everything you generate stays there regardless of what you publish. Only the works you choose to publish appear in Community and on your profile.
