## Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Mixed Media?

Mixed Media is a creative workflow that allows you to combine different types of media - images, videos, and AI-generated content - into a single cohesive project.

What formats are supported?

We support a wide range of image formats (PNG, JPG, WebP) and video formats (MP4, MOV, WebM) for both input and output.

Can I use my own assets?

Yes! You can upload your own images and videos to combine with AI-generated content for a truly unique creative experience.

What is the maximum file size?

Individual files can be up to 100MB for images and 500MB for videos. Premium users have access to higher limits.
