## Got any questions left?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Higgsfield Explainer?

It's a tool that turns a script into a long-form, narrated 2D video. You give it a story or outline, pick a voice, and it generates a fully animated video that stays consistent from the first frame to the last.

How is it different from other AI video tools?

Most generators drift over time — characters morph, the art style shifts, the voice changes between shots. Higgsfield Explainer is built to hold the thread: same characters, same 2D style, and one voice across the entire video.

How long can the videos be?

Up to 10 minutes — long enough for full episodes, explainers, audiobooks, and short films, not just short clips.

What aspect ratios does it export?

Every story renders in both 9:16 (vertical, for Reels, Shorts and TikTok) and 16:9 (widescreen, for YouTube and film) from a single generation.

Can I keep the same characters across a series?

Yes. Characters and style stay consistent, so you can build an episodic series where everything remains recognizable from video to video.

How does the narration work?

You choose a single narrator voice, and it stays consistent through the whole video — no tonal jumps or re-casting mid-story.

What kind of input does it need?

A script or an outline. You set the tone, characters, and 2D style, then generate.

Who is it for?

Storytellers, educators, brands, and creators — anyone making bedtime stories, explainers, social series, product stories, audiobooks, or short films.

What art style does it produce?

A consistent 2D illustrated look that you define up front and that holds across the entire piece.

How do I get started?

Write your story, pick a voice, generate, and export both formats — in minutes.
