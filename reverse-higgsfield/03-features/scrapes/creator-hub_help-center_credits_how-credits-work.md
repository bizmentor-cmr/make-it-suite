# How do credits work?

Copy page

HiggsfieldAug 3, 20264 min

Credits are Higgsfield's currency for generating images and videos. Every generation costs a certain number of credits depending on the model, resolution, and duration, and the exact cost is shown on the Generate button before you confirm. Subscription credits reset regularly and don't roll over: on monthly plans with each paid renewal, on annual plans every 30 days. If you cancel, purchased credits stay available on the free plan until their validity period ends.

## What types of credits exist?

| Credit type | Source | Resets? | Expires? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription credits | Included in your plan | Yes, each billing cycle | Yes, unused credits expire at renewal |
| Credit Pack credits | One-time purchase | No | Yes, 90 days from purchase |
| Auto-Refill credits | Automatic top-up | No | Yes, 90 days from purchase |
| Promo credits | Promocodes or compensation | No | Yes, on the date shown when issued |
| Boost Credits | Included with Concurrency Boost packs, shared across all models | No | Yes, 90 days from purchase |

What types of credits exist?

For full detail on expiry and what happens if you cancel, see [Do my top-up credits expire, and what happens if I cancel?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/top-up-credits-expire)

## In what order are credits spent?

Subscription credits are always spent first. After that, your remaining credits (Credit Pack, promo, and Auto-Refill) are spent by expiration date: the balance expiring soonest is used first. For example, if your promo credits expire before your Credit Pack credits, the promo credits are spent first. The system switches automatically. The same order applies on the free plan after cancellation: with no subscription credits in the balance, purchased credits are simply spent by expiration date.

The practical difference between the two top-ups: a Credit Pack is a one-time purchase you make when you need extra credits right now, while Auto-Refill tops up your balance automatically whenever it drops below the threshold you set, so you never run out mid-project. See [How do Credit Packs work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-credit-packs-work) and [How does Auto-Refill work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-does-auto-refill-work)

## What spends credits?

Credits are deducted for all generations on web, MCP, CLI, Canvas, and Supercomputer: images, videos, re-rolls, and upscaling.

What doesn't deduct credits: browsing the platform, plus Unlimited model generations and the free generations included in your plan. Both apply only on the Higgsfield website ( [higgsfield.ai](https://higgsfield.ai/)): any generation made outside it, including through MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, and other automated tools, always deducts credits, regardless of plan.

## What are free generations?

Some plans include free generations on specific models: a one-time pool granted when you purchase the plan.

For example, a set number of generations may be provided for Soul 2.0 and Soul Cinema without consuming credits. Free generations do not renew upon your subscription renewal. The exact pools are listed in the Unlimited & Free Gens section of your plan's card on the Pricing page. Free generations follow the same rule as Unlimited access: [higgsfield.ai](https://higgsfield.ai/) only.

## How do I check my credit balance?

Your credit balance is visible in the top navigation bar on [higgsfield.ai](https://higgsfield.ai/). For a detailed breakdown, click your avatar and open **Manage Account** → **Subscription**: the Credits card shows your monthly balance and Auto-Refill credits separately.

## How do I check my credit usage history?

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner and select **Manage Account**.
2. Open **Usage** in the left sidebar.

Credits Usage History shows a full log of every credit operation on your account: each generation with the model used and credits deducted, credit returns from failed generations, and a per-model breakdown of your spending. Use it to understand where your credits went or to confirm that a refunded generation returned its credits. The log covers activity from the date the feature was enabled on your account, so operations before that date may not appear.

## Why didn't my credits renew?

Subscription credits reset at renewal, so if your balance didn't update, check the following:

1. **Your reset date hasn't arrived yet.** On monthly plans, credits reset once the monthly renewal invoice is paid. On annual plans, credits reset every 30 days rather than on the same calendar date, so the reset day shifts over time: if you subscribed on May 11, the next reset lands on June 10. Check your renewal date in **Manage Account** → **Subscription**.
2. **The renewal payment failed.** If the charge didn't go through, the subscription enters a past due state and new credits aren't added until payment succeeds. See [What should I do if my payment failed?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/billing/what-should-i-do-if-my-payment-failed)
3. **The subscription was canceled.** After cancellation, credits don't renew: subscription credits remain available only until the end of the paid period.

If none of these apply and your credits still haven't renewed, contact Support with your account email.

## Got any questions left?

How do I know how many credits a generation will cost?

The credit cost is shown on the Generate button before you confirm.

Do unused subscription credits roll over?

No. Subscription credits expire at the end of each billing cycle.

Can I use free generations through MCP or CLI?

No. Free generations work only on [higgsfield.ai](https://higgsfield.ai/): outside it, generations always deduct credits. See [What are Unlimited models and which plans include them?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/what-are-unlimited-models-and-which-plans-include-them)

Do I get my credits back if a generation fails?

Usually yes: the refund is automatic and lands within a few minutes. Specific models might not refund credits for failed generations. You can confirm the refund in **Manage Account** → **Usage**.
