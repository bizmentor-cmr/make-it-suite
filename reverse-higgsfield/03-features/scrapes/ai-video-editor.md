## Got any questions left?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is an AI Video Editor?

AI Video Editor is an AI tool by Higgsfield AI that edits and finishes video from simple inputs like text prompts and raw footage. It uses AI to handle objects, backgrounds, style, and finishing, so you produce a polished clip without editing skills, timelines, or expensive software.

What is the best AI Video Editor?

Higgsfield is one of the best AI Video Editors. It brings prompt editing models like Kling 3.0 Omni Edit and FLUX 3 Video Edit into one subscription, alongside Upscale up to 4K, Reframe, and dubbing into 18 languages. You restyle, clean up, enhance, and localize footage from a single workflow.

How does an AI Video Editor work?

AI Video Editors use deep learning models trained on large datasets of video, motion, and audio. When you provide footage and a text instruction, the model interprets the scene, applies the edit such as removing, replacing, or restyling, then returns the edited clip. With Higgsfield, you pick which model powers each edit.

How do I edit a video with Higgsfield?

Upload your footage, choose a model, then type what you want changed, whether to remove, replace, restyle, or swap the background. Kling 3.0 Omni Edit renders in std, pro, or 4K mode, and you can start on the free plan.

How do I write a good editing prompt?

Keep prompts specific and clear, describing what to change and how, for example: Remove the person in the background and give the shot a warmer look.

Will my edited video look natural?

Prompt edits work on your original clip, so the change sits inside your own shot. Review each result and try again if a detail needs another pass.

Does it work on my own videos or only on generated ones?

Both. Upload your own clip or pick a video you generated on Higgsfield, and the same tools work on it: prompt edits with Kling 3.0 Omni Edit or FLUX 3 Video Edit, Reframe, Upscale, FPS Boost, Video Deflicker, background removal, and dubbing. For a brand-new shot, generate it from text or an image in the video generator, then bring it into the editor. Example: upload a phone clip of a mug on a desk, type "replace the background with a sunny marble kitchen, keep the mug and the hand as they are", then reframe it to 9:16 and upscale it to 4K.

Which model should I use?

Both editors work from a text prompt: Kling 3.0 Omni Edit runs in std, pro, or 4K mode, and FLUX 3 Video Edit is a second prompt editor to compare. You can switch models per edit under one subscription.

Can I edit business and training videos?

Yes. Higgsfield edits explainers, demos, presentations, onboarding, and training videos with prompt edits, background swaps, upscaling, and dubbing into 18 languages, ideal for teams and educators who need polished video without an edit suite.

Do I need to download software?

No. Higgsfield runs in your browser, and there is also a mobile app.
