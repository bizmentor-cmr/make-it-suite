How can I earn money with AI generated videos?

The fastest way to earn is by joining the Higgsfield Earn program. We connect AI creators directly with paid campaigns. Simply use our AI tools to generate unique videos, post them to your Instagram, and get paid based on our transparent 3-tier reward system. It turns your AI experiments into a reliable income stream.

How do I join the Higgsfield Earn program?

It's simple. Log in with your email, connect your Instagram account, and complete our instant Bio Verification. You'll receive a unique code (e.g., "Higgs Influencer") to temporarily add to your Instagram bio. Once our system detects it, your account is verified, and you can start earning immediately.

Is Higgsfield Earn free to join?

Yes. Higgsfield Earn is a monetization platform designed to pay you. There are no hidden fees to join the program. We provide the briefs and the rewards; you provide the creativity.

How much can I earn per campaign?

There is an earning cap of $1,000 on the first day, with a maximum lifetime payout of $2,500 per video. You may submit multiple videos per campaign. Submitting higher quantities of quality content that performs well will maximize your total payout.

When and how do I receive my money?

You can track your "Available to claim" balance directly in your dashboard. Once your earnings are finalized (after the Day 7 milestone), you can withdraw your funds using our supported payment methods.

What is an AI Influencer and how do I monetize one?

An AI Influencer is a virtual persona you create and manage. By building a consistent character and verifying your account with us, you can monetize that character's content just like a human influencer.
