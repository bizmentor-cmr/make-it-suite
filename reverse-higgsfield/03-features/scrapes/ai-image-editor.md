AI Image Editor restyles any image into anime, oil, 3D, sketch, and more. The subject stays intact. The look transforms completely.

AI Image Editor uses one photo as a reference for a face in another image — only with the consent of the person in the photo. Describe the change in a prompt. Results depend on the model.

AI Image Editor expands the canvas beyond the original edges. New surroundings generated to match. Any aspect ratio filled naturally.

AI Image Editor wipes text and captions from your own images. The background rebuilds underneath. Clean and ready to repurpose.

## Got any questions left?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is an AI image editor?

AI Image Editor is an AI tool by Higgsfield that edits any photo from simple inputs like a prompt or reference image. It handles object removal, background changes, retouching, and enhancement, so you get professional results without editing skills or complex software.

What is the best AI image editor?

Higgsfield is one of the best AI image editors. It brings 15+ top models, including Nano Banana Pro, Seedream, FLUX, GPT Image, and Higgsfield Soul, into one subscription, with brush-to-edit on Nano Banana 2, Seedream 5.0 Pro, and Ideogram and up to 4K output on Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, and Seedream 4.5. You edit, restore, retouch, and restyle any image from a single prompt or reference.

How do AI image editors work?

AI image editors use deep-learning models trained on large datasets of images. When you upload a photo and describe a change, the model edits the image to match your prompt and keeps the rest as close to the original as it can. With Higgsfield, you pick which model powers each edit.

How do I edit an image with Higgsfield?

Upload your photo and describe the edit in plain language, or paint a mask over the area on models that support it, then export. Higgsfield generates a polished result, with a free plan to start.

How do I write a good editing prompt?

Keep it specific and clear, describing what to change and how, for example: "remove the person on the left and fill the background naturally."

Will my edited photo look natural?

Edits blend with the original lighting and perspective. Use edits responsibly and follow the rules of the platforms where you publish.

Can Higgsfield edit and generate images?

Yes. Higgsfield both edits existing photos and generates new ones. You can remove, replace, restore, and retouch, then create fresh visuals from a prompt, all in one workflow.

Which model should I use?

It depends on your goal. Nano Banana 2 is best for brush edits, Nano Banana Pro for object swaps and reference-led edits, Seedream for ultra-realistic photo edits, and FLUX or GPT Image for precise, prompt-led changes. You can switch models per edit under one subscription.

Can I edit product and e-commerce images?

Yes. Higgsfield removes backgrounds, adds shadows, and generates scenes for product photos from a prompt, keeping a consistent, marketplace-ready style, ideal for sellers and DTC brands without a photoshoot.

Do I need to download software?

No. Higgsfield runs in your browser, and there is also a mobile app. You get daily free credits.
