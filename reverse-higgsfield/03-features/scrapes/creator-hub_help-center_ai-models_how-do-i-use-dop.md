# How do I use DoP?

Copy page

HiggsfieldAug 1, 20263 min

DoP is how Higgsfield handles cinematic image-to-video. It's Higgsfield's own proprietary model: give it a single image, pick a preset that defines the camera move or effect, and it generates a coherent, directed shot.

## What is Higgsfield DoP?

DoP (Director of Photography) is Higgsfield's native image-to-video model, built for high-quality, controllable, cinematic video generation from images. It's cinematography-aware: trained to understand and control motion, lighting, lens behavior, framing, and spatial composition, so the output reads as a directed shot rather than an animated frame.

The core of DoP is its preset system: each preset is a motion blueprint that defines the camera logic or effect of the clip, from classic camera moves to VFX.

## How do I generate with DoP?

Open [higgsfield.ai](http://higgsfield.ai/) → **Video** → **Create Video**, and select a Higgsfield DoP preset (the model appears as **Higgsfield Standard** in the model selector).

1. **Add your keyframe image.** Upload a PNG or JPG, paste from the clipboard, or generate the image right there. A sharp, well-composed image works best: DoP builds the entire shot from it.
2. **Pick a preset.** Browse by category: **Effects**, **Basic Camera Control**, **Epic Camera Control**, **Catch the Pulse**, or the **New** and **Trending** feeds. Presets marked **Top Choice** are curated highlights, and **Mix** lets you combine presets in one generation.
3. **Describe the scene in the prompt**, with details. The **Enhance** toggle expands your prompt automatically.
4. **Adjust Advanced settings if needed:** clip duration (3 or 5 seconds), seed, and steps.
5. **Generate.** The credit cost is shown on the Generate button before you confirm.

To generate the keyframe itself without leaving the flow, use the **generate it** option in the image input, or prepare it in an image model first: Soul for the signature cinematic look, or Nano Banana Pro for precision. See [How do I use Soul to generate images?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/how-do-i-use-soul-to-generate-images)

## What is DoP best for?

- **Cinematic video from keyframes** — turning a strong still into a directed shot with intentional camera work.
- **Creative concepts and storytelling** — expressive sequences where scene evolution matters.
- **Ads, promos, and branded visuals** — polished motion for commercial assets.
- **Image-driven video pipelines** — workflows where stills are generated first and animated second, including Popcorn storyboards. See [How do I use Popcorn?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/how-do-i-use-popcorn)

## How do I get more accurate outputs?

- **The clip looks soft or blurry.** Start from a sharper, well-composed keyframe: DoP builds the entire shot from it.
- **The motion isn't what you wanted.** Pick a different preset or category: the preset defines the camera logic of the clip.
- **The scene barely changes or ignores details.** Add specifics to the prompt or turn on the Enhance toggle to expand it.

## Got any questions left?

Is DoP a Higgsfield model?

Yes. Higgsfield DoP is Higgsfield's own proprietary model, alongside Soul, Popcorn, and Cinema Studio. See [Which AI model should I use?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/which-ai-model-should-i-use)

Can DoP generate video from text only?

DoP is an image-to-video model: it needs a keyframe image as input. Upload one, paste it from the clipboard, or use the generate it option in the image input.

What do the preset categories mean?

Effects are VFX-driven transformations, Basic and Epic Camera Control are camera moves of increasing intensity, Catch the Pulse is a curated themed collection, and Mix combines presets. New and Trending surface the latest and most-used presets.

Do DoP generations use credits?

Yes, at standard rates, with the cost shown on the Generate button. See [How do credits work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-credits-work)

Can I use DoP presets through MCP?

Yes. The Higgsfield preset catalog for image-to-video is available through MCP, so agents like Claude can browse presets and generate with them. See [What is Higgsfield MCP, and how is it different from the web?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/integrations/what-is-higgsfield-mcp)
