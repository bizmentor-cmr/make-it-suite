## Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Soul Cinema?

Soul Cinema is a new AI image model from the Soul family, designed to generate cinematic-grade images with rich textures, natural compositions, and a distinct mood in every frame.

How is Soul Cinema different from Soul 2.0?

While Soul 2.0 focuses on high-quality aesthetic visuals, Soul Cinema is built specifically for cinematic output - deeper textures, natural grain, and compositions that look like real film stills.

Can I use Soul ID and Soul HEX with Soul Cinema?

Yes. Soul Cinema works with both Soul ID for character consistency and Soul HEX for precise color control across your generations.

What is Soul Cinema best at?

The model excels at close-up shots, mood-driven scenes, and frames with cinematic depth. It's especially strong at capturing textures, natural lighting, and era-specific aesthetics.

Can I use Soul Cinema images for video generation?

Absolutely. Soul Cinema frames work as high-quality keyframes for video models, giving you a cinematic starting point for motion content.

How much does Soul Cinema cost?
