## Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Higgsfield Soul?

Soul is Higgsfield's foundation image model built for creative, fashion-aware, culture-native generation. It produces realistic images with built-in aesthetic taste - designed to feel shot, not generated.

What is Soul ID?

Soul ID lets you create a consistent digital character from your own photos. Train once, then generate unlimited visuals that keep your identity locked across every style, pose, and lighting setup.

How many photos do I need to train Soul ID?

You need a minimum of 20 photos. Training takes about 3 minutes, and once complete, your Soul ID is ready for unlimited generations.

What are presets?

Presets are curated aesthetic styles that instantly set the mood and visual direction of your generation. Instead of writing complex prompts, pick a preset and let Soul handle the rest.

Does Soul support different generation modes?

Yes. Soul supports text-to-image generation and image reference. Start from a text prompt or use an existing image as a style and composition reference to guide your output.

Can I use Soul-generated images commercially?

Yes. All images generated are available for commercial use.
