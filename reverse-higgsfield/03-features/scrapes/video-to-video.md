## Got any questions left?

We've answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Video to Video AI?

Video to Video AI is an AI tool by Higgsfield that transforms your existing video into a new version: it restyles it, swaps objects and outfits, or replaces the background, so you refresh a clip without a reshoot. Need to extend, upscale, or reframe a clip instead? Use AI Video Editor on Higgsfield. No footage yet? Use Text to Video AI.

What is the best Video to Video AI?

Higgsfield is one of the best Video to Video AI tools. It brings Kling 3.0 Omni Edit and FLUX 3 Video Edit into one subscription next to top generators like Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.5, and Veo 3.1. Upscale the result to 4K with Higgsfield Upscale.

How does Video to Video AI work?

Video to Video AI uses deep-learning models trained on large video datasets. When you upload a clip, the model reads the motion and composition, then regenerates only what you want changed while preserving the rest. With Higgsfield, you pick which model powers each transformation.

How do I transform a video with Higgsfield?

Open Video Edit, upload your clip, describe the change, then download. There is a free plan to start.

Can I restyle a video into anime, claymation, or photoreal?

Yes. Describe the style in your prompt, such as anime, 3D, claymation, oil paint, or cinematic film, and Video Edit restyles the clip while keeping its motion and composition.

Can I swap an object, outfit, or background in a video?

Yes. Higgsfield replaces products, clothing, and backgrounds in your clip while keeping the original motion and lighting natural, ideal for refreshing footage without reshooting.

Can I extend a video past its original end?

Yes. Cinema Studio 4.0 extends your clip forward or backward, generating extra seconds that match the motion, scene, and pacing of the original.

Which model should I use?

To edit your own clip, use Kling 3.0 Omni Edit or FLUX 3 Video Edit. Genjutsu transfers motion, Lipsync Studio syncs lips to new audio, and generators like Veo 3.1 and Seedance 2.0 create new shots. You can switch models per transformation.

Will the restyled video look natural and consistent?

In most clips, yes. Higgsfield keeps the original motion, composition, and pacing while it applies the new style or element. If some flicker remains, run the result through Video Deflicker.

Do I need to download software?

No. Higgsfield runs in your browser, and there is also a mobile app.
