# How do I create my first generation?

Copy page

HiggsfieldJul 30, 20262 min

Choose Image or Video from the top navigation, select a model or feature, enter a prompt or upload a reference, and click Generate. Your result appears in the section where you generated it and in Assets.

## How to create your first generation

1. In the top navigation, choose **Image** or **Video**, depending on what you want to create.
2. Select a model or feature. Each section shows what is available. Pick based on your goal, or start with the default.
3. Enter a prompt describing what you want to create.
4. Upload a reference image if the model supports it.
5. Adjust any settings, such as duration, aspect ratio, or resolution.
6. Click **Generate**.

Your generation appears in Assets when it's done.

Not sure which model to use? See [Which AI model should I use?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/which-ai-model-should-i-use)

## What is a prompt?

A prompt is a text description of what you want the AI to create. The more specific, the better. For the full guide with examples, see [How do I write a good prompt?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/getting-started/how-do-i-write-a-good-prompt)

Example **video** prompt:

A close-up of a dancer mid-spin on a rooftop at golden hour, slow motion, cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field.

## How long does generation take?

Usually a few minutes. Images are usually faster, while video can take longer, especially at higher resolutions. If a generation seems stuck, see [My generation is stuck or failed. What should I do?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/troubleshooting/generation-is-stuck-or-failed)

## Where do my generations appear?

Your generations appear in the section where you generated them, and also in Assets, where all your content is collected in one place.
