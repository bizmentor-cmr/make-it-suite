# What's in my Higgsfield account?

Copy page

HiggsfieldJul 30, 20264 min

Your Higgsfield account has three areas. The account menu, which opens when you click your avatar in the top right corner, shows your remaining credits and shortcuts to purchases, your profile, and settings. Manage Account at [https://higgsfield.ai/me/settings](https://higgsfield.ai/me/settings) is where you control your subscription, credits and promocodes. Your public profile at higgsfield.ai/@username is the page other creators see, with your published works, followers, and likes.

## How do I open my account menu?

Click your profile picture in the top right corner of any Higgsfield page. The menu shows your username, current plan, and remaining credits, followed by quick actions:

| Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Credits** | Shows your remaining credit balance |
| **Top-up credits** | Buy additional credits with a Credit Pack |
| **Boost speed** | Add Concurrency Boost to run more generations at once |
| **Unlimited models** | Get access to Unlimited models |
| **View profile** | Opens your public creator page |
| **Manage Account** | Opens your account settings |
| **Increase Concurrent** | Opens the same Concurrency Boost purchase as Boost speed |
| **Join Community** | Opens the Higgsfield Discord |
| **Sign Out** | Signs you out of your account |

How do I open my account menu?

Boost speed and Increase Concurrent are two entry points to the same product: Concurrency Boost. See [How does Concurrency Boost work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-does-concurrency-boost-work)

## What can I do in Manage Account?

Manage Account is your settings hub. The left sidebar has five sections:

| Section | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Personal profile** | Your display name, email address, and profile picture |
| **Gifts** | Gifts you've received or can claim |
| **Subscription** | Your current plan, billing cycle, and renewal settings |
| **Usage** | Your credit usage history |
| **Promocode** | Where you redeem promocodes |

What can I do in Manage Account?

The main page also shows your credit balance with a Top-up button, a Usage history chart of your credit spending over time, and a Collab karma history section. To change your plan or check renewal dates, see [How do Higgsfield plans work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/how-do-higgsfield-plans-work) and [When does my subscription renew?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/when-does-my-subscription-renew) To redeem a code, see [How do promocodes work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-promo-codes-work)

## What is my public profile?

Your public profile lives at higgsfield.ai/@username. Anyone can open it, follow you, and like your work. The profile shows your followers, likes, and three tabs: All works, Projects, and Generations.

Click **Edit profile** to change your picture or username, add a bio, and link your social accounts.

Only content you publish appears on your profile. Everything else stays private in Assets. Works that collect a lot of likes can be featured on the [Higgsfield Explore page](https://higgsfield.ai/), where the whole community sees them.

## What is Assets?

Assets is your private library at [higgsfield.ai/asset/all](http://higgsfield.ai/asset/all). Every image, video, and audio file you create on Higgsfield is saved there automatically, regardless of which tool or model you used. Open it from **Assets** in the top navigation bar, and use the left sidebar to filter by content type: Image, Video, or Audio.

## How do I organize my generations?

Create folders to group related content into projects and move assets into them with the **Move to** option. To keep an asset within quick reach, click the heart icon to add it to Favorites. Folders and Favorites appear in the left sidebar, and a search bar at the top helps you find assets by keyword.

## What can I do with a single asset?

Open any asset to see the full view. From there you can copy the prompt that generated it and see generation details including model, resolution, and creation date.

- **Edit the image** — Color Grading, Upscale, Enhancer, Relight, Inpaint, and Angles are available directly from the asset view
- **Continue working with it** — click **Turn to video** to animate the image, **Recreate** to run the same prompt again, or **Reference** to use the asset as input for a new generation
- **Download and share** — save the file or share it via link and social media
- **Add comments** — leave notes directly on the asset for collaborators

To work with several assets at once, select them and use the bulk action bar at the bottom: Download, Publish all, Add to a folder, Like, or Delete.

## How do I publish an asset?

Open the asset, click the three-dot menu, and select **Publish**. Published assets become visible on your public Higgsfield profile. See [**How do I join the Higgsfield community on Discord?**](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/getting-started/higgsfield-community-on-discord)

## Can I create an Element from an asset?

Yes. Open the asset, click the three-dot menu, and select **Create Element**. This saves the asset as a reusable reference element for future generations. See [How do I create and use a Soul ID character?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/how-do-i-create-and-use-a-soul-id-character)

## Got any questions left?

What's the difference between View profile and Manage Account?

View profile opens your public creator page that anyone can see. Manage Account opens your private settings: subscription, credits and promocodes.

Can other users see my credit balance or subscription?

No. Your balance, plan, and settings are private. Other users only see what you publish on your profile.

How do I redeem a promocode?

Go to Manage Account, select **Promocode** in the sidebar, and enter your code. See [How do promocodes work?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-promo-codes-work)

Can team members see each other's assets?

Other members can't see your assets, but workspace owners and admins can. Admins manage the workspace and may access members' content and settings. On Enterprise plans, this includes tools for viewing assets across the organization. See [What team and business plans does Higgsfield offer?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/business/team-and-business-higgsfield)

What happens to my assets if I cancel my subscription?

Your assets remain accessible while your subscription is active. Canceling turns off auto-renewal and doesn't delete your account or your generations. If you want to remove everything, that's a separate action, see [How do I permanently delete my account?](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/how-do-i-permanently-delete-my-account)

Where do I find the generation ID?

Open the generation in Assets and copy the ID from the page URL: it's the last part of the link.
