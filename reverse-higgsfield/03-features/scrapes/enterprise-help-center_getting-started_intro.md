# Introducing Ourselves

Copy page

Last updatedOct 6, 2026

From your Enterprise Studio contract to an active workspace and your team's first generation.

Your account manager activates the workspace with the credits agreed in your contract. Seats are unlimited.

## How onboarding works

1. **Workspace is ready.**

After the contract and first payment, your account manager delivers a ready workspace with your credit allocation.
2. **Team gets access.**

Invite people from **Manage Account → Members → Invite members**. They receive an email and land in the workspace. SSO can be set up later by your IT team - see [SSO, Guardrails & Security](https://higgsfield.ai/enterprise-help-center/manage-account/sso-security).
3. **Connect with us.**

Ask for a shared Slack or Microsoft Teams channel, or email [enterprise@higgsfield.ai](mailto:enterprise@higgsfield.ai). Average response time: 15 mins.
4. **Start creating.**

Open **Image** or **Video**, pick a model, type a prompt, click **Generate**. The result lands in **Assets**.

## Who changes what

- **Sales representative** \- contract, credit volume, pricing and commercial terms.
- **Customer Success Manager (CSM)** \- your account manager: onboarding, workspace setup, day-to-day questions.
- **Your admins** \- members, groups, limits and permissions.
- **Your IT team** \- SSO setup, together with the workspace Owner.
- **AI Educator** \- workflow training and model choice, arranged through your CSM.

## A good first day

1. Sign in through your invite - on the top right corner, hover over your profile icon, and switch to your new Enterprise workspace .
2. Run one simple generation in the **Image** generator to see the flow end to end.
3. Give the **Supercomputer** agent a real task - a reel, an ad variant, a product shot set.
4. Create a project so results land in one place: **Cinema Studio → + New project** or **Marketing Studio → +**.

**Then**

Contact [enterprise@higgsfield.ai](mailto:enterprise@higgsfield.ai) or your shared Slack or Microsoft Teams channel. Continue with [Onboarding](https://higgsfield.ai/enterprise-help-center/getting-started/onboarding) for your kickoff call and hands-on session.
