## Got any questions left?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What makes App Builder different from other AI builders?

Your app can generate photos, videos and 3D through Higgsfield models out of the box — so you ship a generative product, not just a website. The agent wires the models in automatically, with no API keys or integration code.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe the app in a chat, and the agent handles design, code, database, sign-in and model integration. You can view every change it makes, but you never have to touch the code.

What can I actually build?

Anything from a landing page or portfolio to a full web app with logic, a database and account sign-in — an AI photobooth, an anime filter, a "see your future kid" app, or your own generative tool to share with anyone.

How do I make changes after the app is built?

In the same chat. Say "make it dark" or "add a gallery" and the agent updates the app — you'll get a fresh preview link in minutes.

Who pays when people use my app?

Not you. Users sign in with their own Higgsfield account and generate on their own credits, so a thousand users cost you zero.

What does building cost me?

Builds spend your regular credits — text plus image or video generations if your app includes them. You can create up to 20 sites a day.

How many apps can I publish?

25 publishes on Starter, 250 on Plus, 500 on Ultra and 1,000 per seat on Business.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, from the Plus plan — where you can also remove the "Made in Higgsfield" badge. By default your app lives at name.higgsfield.app.

What happens at publish?

SEO optimization and a security check run in one click, and a cover, icon and description are generated automatically (you can change them). You can also list your app on the Higgsfield marketplace so others can find and remix it.

Can I build outside the chat?

Yes — the same builder is available through the MCP, so power users can create apps straight from Claude or Cursor.
