# Higgsfield Photodump Studio: Your Viral 26-Shot Photoshoot

And for the first time ever: **you can train your first character completely free and dominate your feed.**

* * *

**How to Use Photodump Studio**

**Step 1 – Go to Photodump Studio**

Open [higgsfield.ai/](https://higgsfield.ai/photodump) photodump-studio to begin.

**Step 2 – Train your character**

Upload at least 25 photos to train your Soul ID character. For best results, mix different settings, lighting, and angles. Include 3–4 clear front-view shots capturing the face down to the chest.

**Step 3 – Generate your dump**

Click **Generate** and watch your character instantly produce 26 shots - every angle, style, and pose looking like a studio-grade photoshoot.

**Step 4 – View your results**

All dumps are saved automatically to your [**Soul page**](https://higgsfield.ai/image/soul).

**Step 5 – Download & share**

Download your set and drop it straight to social media platforms. Your first photodump will set your feed on fire.
