# Generation Models

Copy page

Last updatedOct 5, 2026

Image, video and audio model families - what each is best at and how to keep characters consistent.

Start with a preset or choose a model for your task.

## Start with presets

- **Viral Presets -** turn a photo into a short video with ready-made effects. Pick a preset, upload your photo, and generate.
- **Soul Photodump** \- create a photo set of your Soul ID character in different outfits and locations. Choose a character and a preset.

## Image models

- **Higgsfield Soul 2.0** \- realistic people, fashion, and lifestyle photos. A good starting point when the person is the main subject.
- **GPT Image 2.5**\- product visuals, campaign images, and precise edits. Choose Flare for faster work or Sunburst for more detailed editing.
- **Nano Banana** \- reference-based images and edits. Try Pro for more detailed instructions, readable text, and layouts.
- **Seedream 5.0 Pro** \- detailed scenes and product visuals, especially when materials such as water, glass, or steam matter.
- **Recraft V4.1** \- logos, icons, and brand graphics. Choose vector output when the artwork needs to scale without losing quality.
- **Flux** \- detailed images and cinematic stills with depth and atmosphere.

## Video models

**Seedance 2.5** \- a starting point for video. Create videos from a prompt or references, with sound generated alongside the picture. Use it for character scenes, product animation, and connected shots. Clips can be up to 30 seconds long. Try Seedance 2.5 Draft to test your idea before creating the final video.

#### Other models to try

- **Kling** \- character motion, action, and multi-shot scenes. **Kling Motion Control** can apply movement from a reference video to a character.
- **Veo** \- cinematic camera moves, wide scenes, and aerial shots.
- Wan - shots where you want to guide both the first and last frame.
- **MiniMax H3** \- combine character images, motion references, and audio in one video, with dialogue and sound effects.
- **Grok Imagine** \- creative video clips with synchronized sound.

## Video editing and 3D

- **Genjutsu** \- edit an existing video. Use Motion Transfer to apply its movement to a new character or scene. Use Object Swap to replace a product, outfit, or other element.
- **3D Jutsu** \- build an editable 3D scene from a prompt. Position objects, adjust lighting, and plan camera moves before making the final video.

## Audio

- **Voiceover** \- turn your script into speech using a preset or custom voice.
- **Change Voice** \- replace the voice in an existing video.
- **Translate** \- dub a video into another language.
- **Voice Cloning** \- create a reusable voice from your own recording.
- **Seed Audio** \- generate dialogue, music, sound effects, and background sound.
- **Lipsync Studio** \- match a character’s mouth movements to speech.

## Keep characters consistent

Use **Soul ID** for a reusable face identity. Use **Elements** to reference saved characters in supported models.

## Before you generate

Check the credit cost and output settings. Available models and features depend on your plan and workspace permissions.

See [Usage Limits & Spending Control](https://higgsfield.ai/enterprise-help-center/manage-account/limits).
