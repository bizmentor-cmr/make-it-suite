# How to Make Faceless YouTube Videos and UGC Ads with Higgsfield Skills in 2026

HiggsfieldAug 17, 202610 min

![](https://higgsfield.ai/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=webp,onerror=redirect,width=1920,quality=85/https://cdn.sanity.io/images/s479xw3z/production/0952044eda0b3849cca56953e886de2bf56ed59c-2048x1152.png)

Two new skills are now available in Higgsfield MCP and Supercomputer. Type a topic or a product idea into the chat, and a finished video comes back without leaving the conversation. Faceless Content Factory turns a topic into an animated YouTube video up to 10 minutes long. UGC Factory turns a product idea into a creator-led ad with the same avatar carried through the whole clip. This guide covers how to set up MCP for both, a full workflow for each skill, and what it costs to run them.

## What is Faceless Content Factory?

**Faceless Content Factory** takes a topic, a historical event, a how-something-works explainer, a story, and turns it into a full animated video for YouTube, up to 10 minutes long. You pick one of 13 ready visual styles, Stickman Cartoon, Editorial Motion Graphics, Claymotion, and more, then describe the topic. The skill writes the script, generates the narration, builds the animation, adds music, and drops in subtitles, all without you touching a script editor, a voice tool, or an animation program separately.

## What is UGC Factory?

**UGC Factory** does the same kind of thing for product ads. Give it a product idea, a photo of the product, and a creator reference if you have one. It writes an ad concept and a script, then generates a vertical video. The same creator avatar appears consistently from the first frame to the last, across 6 ready templates: Unboxing UGC, Product Review UGC, SaaS UGC, Try-On UGC, Tutorial UGC, and Localization, which keeps the same voice and pacing when the ad needs to run in another language.

Both skills live inside Higgsfield MCP, which means they run right inside a normal ChatGPT or Claude conversation. They're also available directly in Supercomputer on Higgsfield.

- **In Claude:** Copy the connector URL → `https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp`
- → Claude, then Customize → Connectors → name it Higgsfield, paste the URL → connect, sign in, and start.
- **In ChatGPT:** Find Higgsfield in the Plugins Directory → Add → sign in → connect and start creating.

You type what you want in plain language, the same way you'd ask either assistant a question, and a finished video comes back in the same chat.

| Skill | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faceless Content Factory | A topic and a visual style | Animated explainer video, up to 10 minutes, with script, narration, music, and subtitles |
| UGC Factory | A product idea and references | Unboxing, Product Review, SaaS, Try-On, Tutorial, and Localization |

## Which Faceless and UGC Styles to Start With

For Faceless Content Factory, the style you pick sets the tone before a single word of the topic gets written.

- **Stickman Cartoon**, for a topic that should feel simple and approachable, something a kid could follow along with.
- **Editorial Motion Graphics**, best suited to a topic that needs to read more like a documentary or a news breakdown, something with a bit of authority to it.
- **Watercolor Chronicle**, a good fit when the story calls for something softer and more illustrative, a historical moment or a quieter narrative.
- **Fairy Tale & Myth**, built specifically for a myth, a legend, or anything that plays like a fairy tale.
- **Claymotion**, worth reaching for when the story benefits from something tactile and playful, characters that feel handmade instead of flat.

The same logic applies to UGC Factory.

- **Unboxing UGC**, built around that reveal moment, useful when you're launching a new product and want to show it off in detail.
- **Product Review UGC**, for a creator-style product review or demonstration.
- **Try-On UGC**, the right choice when the product needs to be shown worn or used, a piece of clothing, a wearable, anything you'd want on camera instead of just held up.
