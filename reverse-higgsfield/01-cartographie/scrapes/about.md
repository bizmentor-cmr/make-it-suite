## Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Higgsfield?

Higgsfield is the ultimate infrastructure for professional AI video and image creation, on a mission to make cinematic-quality video and image creation accessible to anyone with an idea. The platform serves 25 million users who have generated 850 million generations, including 300 million videos. Higgsfield is backed by Accel, Menlo Ventures, GFT Ventures, and AI Capital Partners at a valuation of over $1.3 billion. The platform is designed for every skill level - from first-time creators to professional filmmakers and Fortune 500 agencies.

What technology does Higgsfield build?

Higgsfield develops proprietary AI models and tools in-house: Soul 2.0 (photorealistic image generation for fashion and editorial), Soul Cinema (cinematic-grade image generation), Higgsfield DOP (cinematic video generation), Keyframes (AI storyboard generation), and Cinema Studio (professional filmmaking workspace with 1,296 camera lenses, cinematic parameters, and color grading), including systems for character consistency (Soul ID) and color control (Soul HEX). The platform's proprietary reasoning engine orchestrates both in-house and partner models – planning narrative structure, camera logic, and visual consistency before any generation happens. This is the cinematic logic layer that makes Higgsfield a full-stack AI infrastructure.

What is Higgsfield's scale?

Higgsfield serves 25 million users and has powered 850 million generations, including 300 million videos. Creators generate 6 million generations per day, including 2 million videos per day. Higgsfield's growth and technology have been covered by Reuters, Forbes, TechCrunch, Adweek, and OpenAI.

What makes Higgsfield different from other AI video tools?

Higgsfield is designed so that creators describe what they want – the platform handles the technical direction automatically. The cinematic logic layer plans every video before generation: narrative arc, camera motion, pacing, and visual emphasis. For beginners this means professional output with zero technical skill. For filmmakers this means Cinema Studio speaks their language with real film terminology and output standards.

What can you create with Higgsfield?

Higgsfield supports the full creative workflow – from social media content for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, to product video ads, AI filmmaking, and enterprise campaign production. Professional teams use the platform for pre-production storyboarding, cinematic trailers, and photorealistic editorial visuals for fashion and lifestyle brands. Over 300 million videos have been created on the platform by creators, marketing teams, and Fortune 500 agencies.
