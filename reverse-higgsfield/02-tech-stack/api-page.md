## Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Higgsfield API?

One key that unlocks 50+ frontier models for image, video, and audio. You send a prompt, we handle the queueing, GPUs, and scaling — you get back a URL.

How does pricing work?

Pay per generation, no subscriptions and no seats. Every model's price is public in the catalog, so you know what a job costs before you run it, and volume discounts apply automatically as usage grows.

What's in the launch offer?

Sign up and get 15% off all models by default. Pick any 4 more models to raise the discount up to 50%, verify your business email and add a card for $15 on your balance, and your first API key comes with 20 concurrent requests.

Do I need to talk to sales to start?

No. The playground is free, and a key takes minutes to create. Sales is only for monthly commitments and custom volume terms.

How hard is the integration?

Three calls: POST the model's endpoint with your prompt, get back a request\_id with a status\_url, poll it until completed — the response carries the file URL. JavaScript, Python, and cURL examples sit side by side in the docs.

I'm already using fal or Replicate. What do I have to rewrite?

Nothing structural — the flow is the same shape: submit, poll, fetch. Most teams switch by swapping a single key in their env.

Can my AI agent use it?

Yes — that's built in. Every model page has a "Copy for agent" setup, there's an MCP server for Claude Code and Cursor, and the docs are published in an agent-readable format.

What are the rate limits?

You start with 20 concurrent requests per key. Raising limits is a settings change, not a manual review — reach out when you need more.

How do I keep spend under control?

Analytics shows spend by day, model, and workflow with discounts visible on the invoice, and exports to CSV for finance. Auto top-up keeps pipelines running; any job can be stopped early via its cancel\_url.

How do teams work?

Personal and team workspaces share one balance and one invoice. Keys are per project — rotate or revoke in one click.

Which models are included?

The catalog covers the top image, video, and audio models — Seedance, Kling, Veo, GPT Image, Soul, VoiceLab and more — and new ones land the day they ship, on the same endpoint, no code changes.
